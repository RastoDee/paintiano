The idea

Music and painting share the same wheel.
Scriabin saw it. Newton saw it.
Now you can hold it in your hand.

Every note gets a colour: hue from its pitch, lightness from its octave, saturation from how hard it was played. Notes drop into a golden-ratio grid — the same proportion behind seashells, flower spirals, the Parthenon. Read the grid back and the music returns. One language, two surfaces.

Questions

Before you tap.

Does it actually work?

Yes. Try the demo above, or tap "Try Paintiano". No signup. No card. Free forever for what most people need.

Why one-time, not subscription?

Because art shouldn't rent. You bought the colours once. We did too. The deal makes sense.

Mobile or desktop?

Both. Mobile is the daily driver — install it from your browser's "Add to Home Screen" and it behaves like a native app. Desktop has the full piano + keyboard shortcuts.

What happens to my data with AI features?

Mood text goes to the AI provider for the call only, then is discarded. No accounts. No tracking pixels. No selling your creations.

I don't know music theory.

You don't need any. Type a feeling, hum, drop a photo. The app handles the chords. It might even teach you a few.

Can I use the paintings commercially?

With Pro or Pro AI, yes. Free exports carry a watermark and are limited to personal use.
