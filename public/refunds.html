If you change your mind within 14 days of buying Paintiano Pro, we will refund you in full. No forms, no justifications — just email us.

1. Your right to a refund

Under EU Directive 2011/83/EU on consumer rights, you have a statutory right to cancel and obtain a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchasing a digital product, even if the product has been delivered.

Paintiano voluntarily applies the same 14-day refund window to all customers worldwide, regardless of where you live.

Note: because Paintiano Pro and Pro AI are digital content delivered immediately, the statutory EU right of withdrawal can end before 14 days once delivery begins and you have agreed to immediate access at checkout (Paddle’s Checkout Buyer Terms). We waive this and still give you the full 14 days below.

2. What's eligible

  • Paintiano Pro Lifetime License — €14.99 (or €9.99 early-bird) — fully refundable within 14 days of purchase.
  • Paintiano Pro AI Lifetime License — €24.99 (or €19.99 early-bird) — fully refundable within 14 days of purchase.

These are the products we sell, so the policy applies to every purchase.

3. How to request a refund

You have two ways to ask:

  1. Email us at hello@paintiano.app from the address you used to purchase. Put "Refund" in the subject line. We will confirm and process the refund within 2 business days.
  2. Contact Paddle support directly — our payment processor handles refunds. They can be reached via the receipt email you received from Paddle, or at paddle.com/contact.

You do not need to give a reason. A simple "I'd like a refund" is enough.

4. How long it takes

  • Our confirmation: within 2 business days of your request
  • Paddle processing: typically 1–3 business days after confirmation
  • Funds back in your account: 3–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer

The refund is issued to the same payment method you used to buy (card, PayPal, etc.). We cannot redirect to a different account.

5. What happens to your license after a refund

After a refund is processed, your Paintiano Pro license key is deactivated. The next time the app validates the key online (typically the next session), Pro features will be turned off and the app will return to the free tier. Your local paintings and creative work are not deleted — only the unlocked features are removed.

6. After the 14 days

Beyond the 14-day window, we are not legally obliged to refund. However, if you have a genuine reason — for example, the app does not work for you due to a bug we cannot fix, or you bought twice by mistake — please email us and we will look at your case individually. We aim to be reasonable.

7. Disputes

If you are not satisfied with how we handled your refund request, you may:

  • Contact Paddle's dispute team directly through the receipt email
  • File a chargeback with your bank or card issuer
  • Bring the dispute to the EU Online Dispute Resolution platform
  • Contact your national consumer protection authority

We prefer to resolve issues directly and amicably — please give us a chance first.
