Whether they exist and how far they go depends on the nature of the product, how it is made available and whether the statutory conditions are met. For digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, the right of withdrawal can end once supply has begun only under the conditions set by law. Different rules may apply to digital services and other kinds of performance. You can exercise your statutory rights through Paddle; you can also contact hello@paintiano.app. These terms do not limit your statutory rights.

Paintiano voluntarily offers a 14-day refund to all customers worldwide, under the fair-use condition in section 2.

2. What's eligible

  • Paintiano Pro Lifetime License — €9.99 — fully refundable within 14 days of purchase.
  • Paintiano Pro AI (lifetime Pro license + AI pass for 12 months) — €19.99 — fully refundable within 14 days of purchase.
  • AI pass for 12 months (for a Pro holder, or as an extension) — €10 — fully refundable within 14 days of purchase; those 12 months of AI are taken back.

These are the products we sell, so the policy applies to every purchase.

Fair use. The voluntary refund is given in full when no more than 5 watermark-free exports (Web and Print together) and no more than 30 generations of AI pieces and melodies together were completed under the licence key. Operations not completed because of a technical fault on the side of the Service do not count. If either limit is exceeded, we look at the request individually and may decline it. For this purpose we keep, per key, counts of these operations — not the content of the works. Your statutory rights are not affected.

3. How to request a refund

You have three ways to ask:

  1. In the app. For 14 days after the purchase, this page opened inside the app (Menu → Refunds) shows the button “Ask for the refund”. It tells you what would be refunded and what would be switched off; a request within the fair use above is passed to Paddle straight away. Any other request is not declined there — the app points you to our e-mail.
  2. Email us at hello@paintiano.app from the address you used to purchase. Put "Refund" in the subject line. We will confirm and process the refund within 2 business days.
  3. Contact Paddle support directly — our payment processor handles refunds. They can be reached via the receipt email you received from Paddle, or at paddle.com/contact.

You do not need to give a reason. A simple "I'd like a refund" is enough.

4. How long it takes

  • Our confirmation: within 2 business days of your request
  • Paddle processing: typically 1–3 business days after confirmation
  • Funds back in your account: 3–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer

The refund is issued to the same payment method you used to buy (card, PayPal, etc.). We cannot redirect to a different account.

5. What happens to your license after a refund

After a refund is processed, your Paintiano Pro license key is deactivated. The next time the app validates the key online (typically the next session), Pro features will be turned off and the app will return to the free tier. Your local paintings and creative work are not deleted — only the unlocked features are removed. A refund does not in itself take away rights that independently belong to you in your own inputs and creations; it does not, however, entitle you to make new paid exports through the deactivated licence.

6. After the 14 days

Beyond the 14-day window, we are not legally obliged to refund. However, if you have a genuine reason — for example, the app does not work for you due to a bug we cannot fix, or you bought twice by mistake — please email us and we will look at your case individually. We aim to be reasonable.

7. Disputes

If you are not satisfied with how we handled your refund request, you may:

  • Contact Paddle's dispute team directly through the receipt email
  • File a chargeback with your bank or card issuer
  • Bring the dispute to the EU Online Dispute Resolution platform
  • Contact your national consumer protection authority

We prefer to resolve issues directly and amicably — please give us a chance first.
