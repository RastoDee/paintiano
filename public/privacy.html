2. The short version

  • Paintiano is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.
  • If you use the free tier without buying anything, we do not receive your email or name.
  • If you buy Paintiano Pro, our payment processor Paddle collects your email + payment details and shares with us only what we need to deliver and validate your license key.
  • We use a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not identify you.
  • AI features send your prompt or a low-resolution image to our AI provider for inference only.
  • We do not sell your data, ever.

5. How long we keep your data

  • License records (email + key) — kept while the license remains active. After refund or deactivation, the email is anonymised within 30 days.
  • Invoices & tax records — kept for 10 years as required by Slovak tax law.
  • Support emails — kept for up to 2 years.
  • AI prompts and image submissions — not stored beyond the request lifetime (used only to generate the response).
  • Anonymous analytics — kept aggregated; no individual identifiers.

6. Cookies and local storage

Paintiano uses your browser's local storage to remember:

  • Your language preference
  • Your AI trial counter (free tier only)
  • Your Pro license key (after purchase)
  • Whether you've seen the onboarding screen

This data stays on your device only. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Our analytics tool runs cookieless.

7. Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, for processing based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint — with the Slovak Data Protection Authority (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR, dataprotection.gov.sk)

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@paintiano.app. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Paintiano is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures: HTTPS everywhere, encrypted storage at the database level, principle of least privilege for API keys, and regular review of our sub-processors' security practices. No system is 100% secure, but we take this seriously.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective" date at the top will be updated. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email (if we have your email) or via a banner inside the Service.
