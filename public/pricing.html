How it works

Paintiano runs entirely in your browser at paintiano.app — no install required (though you can add it to your home screen as a Progressive Web App).

When you buy Paintiano Pro, a license key arrives in your inbox within minutes. Paste it into the app once, and every device you use with that browser unlocks the full experience.

Is it really one-time?

Yes. No subscription, no auto-renewal, no hidden fees. Pay once, use forever.

What happens after I buy?

Our payment processor (Paddle) emails you a license key immediately. Paste it into Paintiano → "I already have a key" → done. Pro features unlock instantly.

Will my price stay €9.99?

The first 50 supporters lock in €9.99 forever. After that, the price moves to €14.99. Your license never expires.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, 14-day no-questions-asked. See our Refund Policy.

Does Paintiano work on iPhone / iPad?

Yes — it's a Progressive Web App that runs in mobile Safari and Chrome. Add it to your home screen for a full-screen experience.

What about VAT?

Prices include any taxes that our payment processor collects automatically based on your country.
