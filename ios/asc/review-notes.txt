Paintiano turns music into a painting: each piece of music produces its own picture. Painting needs no account or sign-in.

New in 1.1 - Battle (the third tab at the top: For You, Studio, Battle):
Battle is a monthly game for paintings made in Paintiano. Two paintings are shown and a double tap votes for one. Voting needs no account. Only sending your own painting to compete needs a Battle account: a nickname and a password, made inside Battle (no e-mail, no third-party sign-in).
Until the first round opens on December 1, 2026, visitors without an account see a countdown screen. To see the game now, sign in with the demo account from the sign-in fields: Battle tab > the circle at the top right > Sign in.
Content from other people in Battle is only paintings made in Paintiano and nicknames (no chat, no free text, no photos):
- The rules are shown when an account is made; offensive nicknames are refused.
- Report: hold a painting > Report / block > Report painting or Report nickname. Reports are looked at within 24 hours; the content can be removed and the account closed.
- Block: hold a painting > Report / block > Block the author. Their paintings are no longer shown to you.
- Delete account: the circle at the top right > Delete account (the password, then a confirmation). The account, its paintings and its votes are deleted.
- Contact: hello@paintiano.app

In-app purchases (StoreKit):
- Paintiano Pro (non-consumable): unlocks all painter styles, composers, custom palette and watermark-free export.
- AI pass 12 months (non-renewing subscription): AI composition from a mood or a photo for 12 months. It does not renew.
To see the purchase screen: tap a locked painter or a locked feature. "Restore purchases" is at the bottom of that screen.

Refunds: the Refunds page (menu "Paintiano" at the top > Refunds) has a button that opens Apple's refund request sheet.

Licence key field: Paintiano is also a web app. People who bought a licence earlier on the website can enter their key to use it here (multiplatform service). All purchases inside the iOS app use In-App Purchase.

AI features: a short text or a downscaled photo is sent to our server to compose a short piano piece. It is not a chat.

Microphone: used only when the user chooses to play or hum a melody; the audio is turned into notes on the device.
