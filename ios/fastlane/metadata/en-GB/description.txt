Paintiano turns music into paintings. Play a song and watch it paint itself, note by note: the chords choose the colors, the melody chooses the shapes, the rhythm sets the brushstrokes. The same song always paints the same painting, on any phone, for anyone.

ONE SONG, 24 PAINTERS
Swipe up and the same notes are painted in another style, inspired by Klimt, Hokusai, Monet, Kandinsky, Mondrian, Matisse and 18 more. Swipe sideways for more versions of the same painter. Fusion mixes 2–4 painters into one brand-new painter.
