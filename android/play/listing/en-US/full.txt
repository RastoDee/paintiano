Every song has its own painting. Paintiano finds it.

Play a piece and watch it appear, note by note. This is not a random visualizer: the music itself chooses the picture — its chords, its keys, its rhythm — so the same song always paints the same painting, on any phone, for anyone.

MUSIC BECOMES PAINTING
• Play a MIDI file, an audio recording or a video, or pick one of the built-in pieces.
• Sing, play or hold the phone to a speaker — the microphone paints live.
• Each of the twelve notes has its own colour on a wheel built on the golden ratio.

PICTURES BECOME MUSIC
• Any photo or painting can be played: its colours are read as a score.
• Or hear it recomposed in the spirit of one of fifteen composer styles.

ONE SONG, MANY HANDS
• See the same notes in 24 painter styles, each with several ways of painting.
• Fusion puts up to five painters on one canvas; a Jam session lets composers trade phrases.
• Swipe up for a new painting, sideways for its variants.

KEEP IT
• Save the painting, share it, or export it print-ready for your wall.
• Your favourites stay on your device.

AI THAT COMPOSES
• Describe a mood, or choose a photo: AI writes a new piece to match, and Paintiano paints it.

FREE, PRO AND THE AI PASS
Paintiano is free to use with 9 painter and 3 composer styles and one free try of every AI feature. Pro is a one-time purchase that opens every style, your own palette and print-quality exports without a watermark — it is yours for good. The AI pass adds AI composing for 12 months; it does not renew by itself.

All paintings and music are Paintiano originals, made from your music and inspired by the styles named in the app — not copies of anyone's work, and not made with or endorsed by any artist or estate.
